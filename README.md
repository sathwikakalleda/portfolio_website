## 📊 Featured Projects

### 1. Adventure Works Sales Analytics Dashboard
> **Domain:** Retail / Sales Analytics · **Tools:** Power BI, DAX, SQL, Star Schema

End-to-end BI solution on the Adventure Works dataset — executive KPI overview, sales trend analysis, product performance, customer insights, and geographic breakdown across 5 dashboard pages with 20+ DAX measures.

🔗 [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/sathwikakalleda/Adventure-Works-Analysis)

---

### 2. Automobile EDA — Pricing & Performance
> **Domain:** Automotive Analytics · **Tools:** Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib, NumPy

Exploratory Data Analysis on 205 automobiles (26 features) to understand how engine size, horsepower, curb weight, and fuel type influence pricing — including univariate, bivariate, and multivariate analysis with a correlation heatmap.

🔗 [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/sathwikakalleda/Exploratory-Data-Analysis)

---

### 3. UAE Banking Data Analysis
> **Domain:** Banking & Financial Services · **Tools:** SQL, CTEs, Window Functions, Star Schema

Simulated UAE retail banking environment with 1,000+ customers and 10,000+ transactions — KPI monitoring, branch performance tracking, customer segmentation by value and risk, credit vs debit liquidity analysis, and compliance-ready reporting. BI-ready star schema designed for Power BI integration.

🔗 [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/sathwikakalleda/Banking-Data-Analysis-UAE-)
